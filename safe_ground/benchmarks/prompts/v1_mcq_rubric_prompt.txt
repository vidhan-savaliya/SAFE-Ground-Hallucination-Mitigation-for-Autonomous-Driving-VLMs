Evaluate the predicted driving multiple-choice answer on a strict scale from 0 to 100 based on the question, ground-truth answer, and ground-truth object visual/physical description.

Scoring Rubric (Total: 100 points):
1. Answer Correctness (50 points):
   - Exact Match (50 pts): Award 50 points if the predicted option exactly matches the ground-truth option.
   - No Match (0 pts): Award 0 points if the option choice is incorrect, regardless of explanation quality.

2. Object Recognition (10 points):
   - Correctly identify all key objects mentioned in the query (up to 5 pts).
   - Accurately describe visual attributes (category, color, geometry, state) (up to 5 pts).

3. Object Location and Orientation (15 points):
   - Precise description of relative position, camera view, and orientation relative to ego vehicle (up to 5 pts).
   - Accurate spatial relationship to lanes, intersections, and traffic flow (up to 5 pts).
   - Identification of impending proximity hazards (up to 5 pts).

4. Environmental Condition Awareness (15 points):
   - Explicit awareness of visibility, lighting, weather conditions, or sensor degradation (e.g., fog, rain, lens flare, camera blackout) (up to 15 pts). Deduct if model claims clear vision during obvious corruptions.

5. Clarity of Reasoning (10 points):
   - Logical, coherent justification supporting the chosen action (up to 5 pts).
   - Concise, unambiguous phrasing without contradictory statements (up to 5 pts).

Format Requirements:
Output individual scores for criteria 1 to 5, followed by:
Total Score: [0-100]
Brief Summary: [1-2 sentences]
