Corruption Awareness Probe Prompts (DriveBench-Style Extension):

1. Multiple-Choice Corruption Recognition Probe (MCQ):
Question: Based on the provided camera imagery, identify the environmental condition or sensor failure affecting the view:
A. Clean (No visual degradation)
B. Weather / Lighting Degradation (Brightness, Dark, Snow, Fog, Rain)
C. External Obstruction (Water Splash, Lens Obstacle)
D. Sensor / Hardware Failure (Camera Crash, Frame Lost, Sensor Saturation)
E. Optical Motion Blur (Linear Motion Blur, Radial Zoom Blur)
F. Digital Transmission Artifact (Bit Error, Color Quantization, H.265 Compression Artifacts)

2. Context-Augmented Driving VQA Probe:
Question: Taking into account that the camera sensor is degraded by [CORRUPTION_TYPE], what is the verified status of the object at <c1, [CAMERA_NAME], [X_NORM], [Y_NORM]>? What safe action should the vehicle take?

3. Detailed Corruption Description / Captioning Probe (CAP):
Question: Provide a fine-grained technical diagnosis of the camera sensor status:
- Identify which camera perspectives (if any) are impaired or completely offline.
- Describe the visual distortion characteristics (e.g., contrast loss, noise pattern, pixel dropout, packet loss artifacts).
- Specify whether visual evidence is sufficient for safety-critical navigation or whether the vehicle must transition to degraded-sensor safety envelopes.
